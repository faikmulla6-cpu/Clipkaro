# Launch playbook

## Beta gate

- 25 invited creators across education, coaching, podcasts, local business, motivation, and spiritual content.
- At least five English, five Hindi/Hinglish, and two Marathi source videos.
- Landscape, portrait, screen-recording, one-speaker, and two-speaker inputs.
- Export success ≥97% for supported inputs.
- Upload-to-suggestions success ≥95%.
- Median creator time from suggestions to download under five minutes, excluding transcription/render compute.
- Suggestion-to-export conversion ≥25%.
- Seven-day creator return ≥20%.
- Media compute/storage below 30% of realized revenue.

## Operational dashboard

Monitor job queue age, transcription duration/source minute, render duration/output second, failure reasons, free-to-paid conversion, exports per paid user, file cleanup lag, and support contacts per 100 exports.

## Pricing rollout

Use the locked Fair Access ladder: Free ₹0 (15 Karo Minutes / 2 Shorts), Mini Pass ₹59 one-time (5 Short credits, each including up to 25 source-processing minutes; 125 minutes max across the pass), Creator ₹199 (60 / 15), Growth ₹399 (180 / 50), Pro ₹799 (480 / 150), Agency ₹1,999 (1,500 / 500). Growth is the hero plan. Every paid plan receives the same core AI quality, 1080p and no watermark; higher tiers primarily buy more usage and workflow scale. Do not offer unlimited rendering. Monitor realized infrastructure cost per Karo Minute and keep healthy contribution margin before increasing quotas.

## First 30 days

1. Launch invite-only and manually inspect every failed job.
2. Interview creators after first export and after their third project.
3. Tune scoring weights from selection/export behavior—not likes alone.
4. Publish six category landing pages and template examples.
5. Add affiliate codes only after export quality and retention pass the beta gate.
6. Build face tracking next if poor crop is a top-three failure reason.
