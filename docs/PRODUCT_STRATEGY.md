# Product strategy

ClipKaro borrows proven interaction patterns, not code or visual identity.

## Reference decisions

- OpusClip demonstrates that creators value ranked clips, score explanations, animated captions, reframing, and reusable brand templates. ClipKaro keeps the score transparent across hook, clarity, value, emotion, and flow rather than presenting a mysterious number. Source: <https://www.opus.pro/> and <https://help.opus.pro/docs/article/virality-score>.
- Vizard validates transcript-led editing, a content calendar, platform captions, and the combination of automation with manual control. ClipKaro ships a focused calendar draft flow while social connections remain explicit and rights-safe. Source: <https://vizard.ai/> and <https://vizard.ai/tools/content-calendar>.
- Klap highlights auto-reframing, speaker focus, strong captions, brand customization, and publish workflows. ClipKaro's launch renderer supports three aspect ratios and brand styling; face tracking is isolated as the next rendering upgrade. Source: <https://klap.app/>.
- Captions shows the value of style-led editing and natural-language simplicity. ClipKaro uses named creator personalities and a compact studio rather than a full timeline. Source: <https://captions.ai/features/edit-with-ai>.

## Why ClipKaro can win

1. India-first affordability without unlimited-loss pricing.
2. Hindi/Hinglish/Marathi-friendly multilingual transcription and India-specific templates.
3. Clear score reasons that teach creators what makes a moment work.
4. Upload-owned-video positioning and automatic expiry.
5. A simple studio: choose moment → style → export.
6. Local-business, educator, coaching, spiritual, and agency use cases—not podcast-only positioning.

## Launch promise

> Upload one video you own. See your strongest moments live. Publish the ones worth sharing.

Avoid unprovable “guaranteed viral” language. The UI calls the metric potential and explains its signals.

## Deferred deliberately

- Authorized YouTube OAuth import
- Face/speaker tracking and split-screen layout
- Direct social posting after account authorization
- Filler-word/silence removal
- AI B-roll and generative assets
- Agency workspaces and approvals
- Mobile app

These are valuable, but they should not delay proof that Indian creators will repeatedly upload, select, and export.
